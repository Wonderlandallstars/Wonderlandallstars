❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What's the minimum age for private lessons?

We accept students ages 5 and up! Younger students may benefit from shorter sessions (30 minutes) while they build focus and attention span.

Can I request a specific coach?

Absolutely! We encourage you to choose the coach whose specialty matches your goals. If your preferred coach is unavailable, we'll help schedule as soon as possible.

Where do lessons take place?

All private lessons are held at our state-of-the-art training facility. We have dedicated spaces for individual coaching to ensure a focused training environment.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made 48 hours in advance receive a full refund or reschedule credit. Late cancellations may incur a fee.

Can siblings share a lesson?

Yes! Group lessons for family members can be arranged at a slightly higher rate. Contact us for details!
