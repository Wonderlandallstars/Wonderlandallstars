ABOUT ENCHANTED LEGACY

Where Magic Meets Athleticism

✨ Our Mission

Enchanted Legacy Cheer is dedicated to building elite athletic champions while inspiring confidence, character, and leadership. We blend the artistry of Disney magic with the intensity of world-class competitive cheerleading.

Our program empowers young athletes to reach their full potential, both on and off the mat. Through rigorous training, creative choreography, and unwavering support, we create an environment where legends are born.

📖 Our Story

Founded with a vision to bring Disney-inspired magic to the competitive cheer world, Enchanted Legacy has grown into a premier Allstar program serving athletes of all levels.

From our first tiny team to our elite Level 6 competitors, every athlete who joins our legacy becomes part of a family dedicated to excellence, creativity, and inclusion.

🏢 Our Facilities

Enchanted Legacy is located in a state-of-the-art training facility equipped with:

  • Professional spring floors designed for tumbling and stunting safety
  • Multiple practice mats and training areas
  • Full-length mirrors for technique refinement
  • Comfortable viewing areas for parents
  • Modern climate-controlled training environment
  • Sound system for routine choreography
